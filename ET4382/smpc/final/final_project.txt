SMPC Final Project 2026

In this project, you will design a buck converter to generate a 1.2V supply from a 3.3V source to power an SoC. The supply ripple should be kept below 100mV peak-to-peak, and the switching node ringing peak value should be kept below 1.5V due to parasitic inductances. The SoC can consume a peak current of 10mA, but during sleep mode, it will consume 10µA. The peak efficiency should be at least 90%. It should also be able to reject an interference signal @ 2kHz from nearby by ~ 40dB. All components from 180 BCD process can be employed.

Design considerations:

Try to minimize the build-of-materials (BOM): try to use small external inductors and capacitors.

Try to optimize the chip area for the same performance.

Try to reach high peak efficiency and analyze efficiency over output power.

Try to minimize power consumption with low load currents.

Consider the impact of parasitic inductances, assume 2nH + 50mΩ for each connection wire from the chip to the PCB.

You should address the following:

System-level design: Selection of switching frequency, inductor, and capacitor, control loop strategy (you are free to choose a control loop other than a type-III compensator if you want).

Selection and sizing of power FETs

Gate driver design and considerations

Efficiency analysis and optimization strategy

Interference rejection strategy

You will be graded based on your understanding and explanation of the aforementioned aspects of your design, not by how good the numbers look.

All the simulation results should have white background and large enough fonts for easy readability.
